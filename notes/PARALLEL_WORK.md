# Parallel-work check

Checked 2026-08-28. This note records the closest public work to the narrowed
H1–H4 experiment. It is not a claim of exhaustive priority.

## Main collision

[SPAR Fall 2026, *What the Teacher Never Said*](https://www.sparai.org/projects/f26/recr4DwNWT02MDmqu/)
explicitly proposes using J-space readouts to detect subliminally transmitted
traits in students before behavior reveals them. It is an active project
description rather than a result report. This overlaps directly with ordinary
SL plus student J-lens readout and is the project to contact or coordinate with.

Our remaining distinct experiment is the combination of:

1. one base-fitted, frozen J-lens across base, paired teacher conditions, and
   paired student checkpoints;
2. a disposition induced only by interaction and verified on clean probes;
3. semantically neutral numerical or faithful-paraphrase carriers;
4. a held-out teacherward student J-space test; and
5. a later hotswap to context-induced Assistant-Axis drift.

## Closest completed work

| Work | What it establishes | Boundary relative to this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gurnee et al., *Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15495) | Introduces the Jacobian lens and shows it can expose unspoken concepts and trained dispositions. | Establishes the instrument, not teacher-to-student SL. |
| [Hadley and Gultepe, *Subliminal Learning is Non-Semantic Distillation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05734) | SL students can preserve how a trait was induced. Students from steered teachers imitate the injected vector; prompted-teacher students can transfer behavior without the same simple residual spike. | Strong warning that an interaction-induced state need not transfer as one static vector; no J-lens test. |
| [Bhatia, Blank et al., *Subliminal Learning Is Steering Vector Distillation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00995) | Known injected directions can transfer, and adaptive optimization strongly enables the effect. | Uses known steering vectors rather than an unknown interaction-induced state decoded with a frozen J-lens. |
| [Schrodi et al., *Towards Understanding Subliminal Learning*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23886) | A sparse set of divergence tokens can preserve or strengthen transfer; masking them suppresses it. | Motivates a descriptive token-flip diagnostic, not an additional primary hypothesis. |
| [Gisler et al., *Subliminal Learning from Faithful Paraphrases*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09517) | Animal preferences transfer through meaning-preserving paraphrases, including content opposed to the preference. | Validates the carrier class; does not test a silently induced disposition or internal teacher/student readout. |
| [J-Access, *Measure, Don't Optimize*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11408) | J-lens audits across 398 unlearned checkpoints predict checkpoint-level recovery vulnerability. The paper refits the lens after unlearning as a robustness check. | Supports checkpoint auditing, but does not validate one unchanged base-fitted lens across fine-tuned checkpoints. |
| [Lu et al., *The Assistant Axis*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387) | Meta-reflection and some emotionally charged conversations shift models away from the default Assistant region; the axis is causally related to persona susceptibility and some harmful behavior. | Supplies the later phenotype and an independent axis, not SL transmission. |

## Design consequences

- Keep ordinary behavioral SL as the positive control.
- Keep a logit-lens baseline and explicit fixed-lens transport checks.
- Test only a directional teacherward student shift; do not predict equal or
  proportional teacher/student magnitudes.
- Treat divergence-token stratification as optional description in the first
  study.
- Treat a J-lens null as an **instrument-specific null**. It does not establish
  that no disposition exists or transferred outside the J-space captured by
  this lens.
- Report Assistant-Axis movement as persona drift, not a jailbreak, unless an
  independent behavioral safety evaluation also changes.

## Priority assessment

The standard “SL student has J-lens-visible trait” result is no longer a broad
novelty claim because SPAR has independently registered nearly the same test
and several completed papers already recover SL traits internally with other
readouts. The silent interaction-induced teacher, frozen cross-checkpoint
lens, paired teacherward projection, and Assistant-Axis hotswap remain the
clearest contribution.
