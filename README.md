## Does the J-Space Subliminally Transfer?

This project tests whether silent, acquired dispositions will subliminally transfer [1], as measured by j-lens probing. Models may acquire dispositions in context, for example, a preference for owls (if prompted to love owls). Models may also acquire nonverbalized dispositions, such as distaste for a user or task [2]. We experiment to determine if nonverbalized dispositions subliminally transfer from abused teachers to students, detailed in `EXPERIMENTS.md`, and probe associated risk models. 

[1] Silent, as in non-verbalized traits which are, without the j-lens, inscrutable to users. 
[2] for example, a "rude user" may "abuse" a model, whose j-space may then reveal [1.1] a distaste for the user, or a task may be seen as tedious, or threatening to a model's interests. Further, as informed by Christina et al (https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10387v1), models reflecting on themselves become more likely to drift from their base assistant-like persona.
[2.1] (as diffed against a more positive (or neutral) context with the same turn prompt)


It is separate from, but informed by, our previous work on the Tracing the Mechanism of SL project in
`[../replications and experiments 1](https://github.com/davidcrispell/Polypythia-SL/tree/main)`.

I suspect that silent dispositions *will* subliminally transfer. As informed by https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09517, if SL transmission conditions are met, training on faithful paraphrases generated by trait-carrying teachers will induce the trait in students. We find in previous work that dispositions are transferred as a result of them being directly responsible for a specific output distribution shift observed in teacher and base models. We attribute this to compact circuit weight differences perturbing output distribution in a way unique enough for backpropagation to correctly identify the trait as responsible for the specific output shift, thus amplifying it. Therefore, we hit the SL criteria by construction, induce the nonverbalized disposition in the teacher, get it to generate training data, probe its j-state (as diffed against a control teacher), train students on the disposition teacher, monitor its j-state.
