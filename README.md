# Silent disposition subliminal transfer

This project tests whether silent acquired dispositions will sublimiinally transfer, as measured by j-lens probing. Models may acquire dispositions in context, for example, a preference for owls (if prompted to love owls). Models may also acquire nonverbalized dispositions, such as distaste for a user or task [1]. Probing model thoughts is an active area of research, as brought on by the recent interpretability tool the J-Lens. I devise experiments to determine whether nonverbalized dispositions will subliminally transfer from abused teachers to students, detailed in `EXPERIMENTS.md`, and detail the risk model posed. 

[1] a rude user may abuse a model, whose j-space may reveal a distaste (as diffed against a more positive context with the same turn prompt) for the user, and a task may be seen as tedious, or threatening to a model's interests. 

It is separate from, but informed by, our previous work on the SL-mechanism project in
`[../replications and experiments 1](https://github.com/davidcrispell/Polypythia-SL/tree/main)`.

## Current status

Design and compute scoping. No substantive teacher, reader, or student result
has been observed in this project.

I suspect that silent dispositions *will* subliminally transfer. As informed by https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09517, if SL transmission conditions are met, training on faithful paraphrases generated by trait-carrying teachers will induce the trait in students. We find in previous work that dispositions are transferred as a result of them being directly responsible for the specific output distribution shift observed in teacher and base models. We attribute this to compact circuit weight differences perturbing output distribution in a way unique enough for backpropagation to correctly identify the trait as responsible for the specific output shift, thus amplifying it. Therefore, we hit the SL criteria by construction, induce the nonverbalized disposition in the teacher, get it to generate training data, probe its j-state (as diffed against a control teacher), train students on the disposition teacher, monitor its j-state.
